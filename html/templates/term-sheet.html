Status

Indicative and non-binding. Subject to contract, due diligence and each party's approvals. Only a signed agreement creates obligations.

  1. Provider

    KC&C (the “Provider”).

  2. Client

    Sample Counterparty Ltd (the “Client”).

  3. Service

    Hourly risk parameters for CC collateral (loan-to-value, liquidation threshold, liquidation bonus, supply cap), signed and delivered to the Client’s receiver contract.

  4. Bounds

    The Client sets a minimum, a maximum and a maximum step per update for each parameter. The receiver applies only values inside the Bounds.

  5. Keys

    Loosening requires two separate Provider keys. Tightening requires one. The Client may pause the Service at any time.

  6. Pilot

    Four weeks in shadow mode. No value is applied to the Client’s pool during the Pilot.

  7. Fees

    Pilot: none. After the Pilot: to be agreed (Schedule 1).

  8. Data

    The Client shares aggregate position data and parameter history. The Provider is never a party to the Client’s transactions.

  9. Confidentiality

    Each party keeps the other’s non-public information confidential for three years after the Term.

  10. Liability

    To be agreed. The Provider publishes data and does not lend; it gives no guarantee of any outcome.

  11. Term

    Twelve months from signature, renewable by agreement.

  12. Governing law

    To be agreed.
